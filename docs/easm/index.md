# External Attack Surface Management Tools

In addition to its [core search features](../search/index.md), Netlas provides a set of attack surface management tools dedicated to both offensive and defensive cybersecurity professionals.

Since Netlas uses a passive scanning method, these tools are designed to manage any external attack surface, supporting use cases such as:

- Discovering and mapping an attack surface
- Uncovering shadow IT
- Minimizing the external attack surface
- Detecting used technologies, software, and security issues, including vulnerabilities
- Gathering information for OSINT reports

Currently, Netlas provides the following eASM tools:

- **[Attack Surface Discovery Tool](discovery.md)**: Assists in mapping out internet-exposed parts of any information system, providing a view of potential points of access, including those in third-party and cloud services.
- **[Netlas Private Scanner](scanner.md)**: Enables you to perform non-intrusive scans of any target, providing the most up-to-date results compared to publicly available scan data.

Netlas external attack surface management tools are actively in development and are subject to improvements in future releases.

## Team Access

Netlas users can collaborate by teaming up to share their attack surfaces and scans within the team. Any team member has read-only access to all attack surfaces and scans created by their teammates. This means they can view and share attack surfaces and scans created by other team members.

To create a team, go to your [profile settings](https://app.netlas.io/profile/) and navigate to the Teams section.

## Availability

Most of Netlas eASM features are available only on paid plans.

- **The availability of some search options in the Discovery Tool depends on data availability restrictions.**  
  For example, if your pricing plan does not provide access to contact details such as phone numbers and email addresses, related WHOIS searches will also be unavailable.

- **The maximum number of nodes per group on the attack surface is limited by your pricing plan.**  
  A search will be unavailable to you if it returns more results than the group capacity limit.

- **Netlas Private Scanner is available only on paid plans.**  
  There are also limitations on the number of hosts that can be scanned per month and the number of targets in a single scan.

Please refer to the pricing table for more information.
